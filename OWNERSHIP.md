# Ownership And Stewardship Notes

## Current copyright owner

As of now, the intended project owner is:

**Vikas Agarwal** (project steward)

This is the simplest starting point for a project that may later support official hosted, managed, support, enterprise, commercial, or dual-licensed offerings.

## Public core commitment

The public bluei core is intended to remain open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

Copies already released under AGPL-3.0-or-later remain under that license. The project owner does not intend to take down or close-source the public core.

## If a company is formed later

If ownership is later moved into a company, the usual path is:

1. Vikas Agarwal forms or designates the legal entity.
2. Vikas Agarwal signs an IP assignment or copyright assignment agreement transferring the project rights to that entity.
3. Future releases can then be published by that company as the copyright owner or project steward.

## Important practical note

What changes is who owns the rights for:
- future releases,
- commercial licensing,
- hosted service terms,
- and relicensing decisions.

The official bluei name, logo, domain, Waterworks HQ branding, and official hosted/commercial offerings are controlled separately from the source-code license. See `TRADEMARK.md`.

## Why the CLA matters

The project's `CLA.md` is intended to ensure that contributions can continue to be used in bluei, including official open-source, hosted, managed, support, enterprise, commercial, or dual-licensed versions, without needing to chase every contributor later.

Contributors retain ownership of their contributions. The CLA grants project-use rights; it is not a copyright assignment.

## Not legal advice

This file is a practical project note, not legal advice. For a major launch or formal entity transfer, use a real assignment agreement reviewed by counsel.
