# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Thank you for contributing to **bluei**.

## Plain-English Summary

bluei's public core is open source under **AGPL-3.0-or-later** and is intended to remain open source.

By contributing, you keep ownership of your contribution, but you give the project owner the rights needed to use that contribution in bluei, including in the public open-source project and in official hosted, commercial, enterprise, support, managed-service, or dual-licensed versions of bluei.

This CLA exists so bluei can grow as an open-source project while leaving room for an official commercial product and future company stewardship.

## Why This Exists

The project owner wants bluei to be useful to individuals, teams, and companies that self-host, modify, study, and improve the software. The project owner also wants the ability to:

- offer an official hosted version in the future,
- enter into commercial, support, managed-service, or enterprise agreements,
- offer commercial licenses where appropriate,
- transfer stewardship of the project to a future company entity.

This CLA is meant to preserve that flexibility without taking ownership of your contribution away from you.

## Individual Contributor Agreement

By submitting a contribution to this repository, including any pull request, patch, commit, issue-attached code, documentation, design, configuration, or other content intended for inclusion, you agree that:

1. **You have the right to submit the contribution.**
   The contribution is your original work, or you have sufficient rights to submit it under these terms.

2. **You retain ownership of your contribution.**
   This CLA does not assign your copyright to the project owner.

3. **You grant project-use rights to the project owner.**
   You grant to **Vikas Agarwal**, and to any future legal entity to which Vikas Agarwal transfers or assigns ownership or stewardship of bluei, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works of, distribute, sublicense, and relicense your contribution as part of bluei or an official bluei-related offering.

   Official bluei-related offerings may include the public open-source project, official hosted services, managed deployments, support offerings, enterprise editions, commercial licenses, dual-licensed distributions, documentation, websites, examples, and project tooling.

4. **Open-source distribution remains allowed.**
   You agree that your contribution may be distributed as part of bluei under the repository's open-source license, currently **AGPL-3.0-or-later**.

5. **Patent license.**
   To the extent you hold patent rights covering your contribution, you grant a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free patent license for those rights as necessary to use, modify, distribute, host, sell, support, and otherwise provide bluei and official bluei-related offerings.

6. **Moral rights waiver/consent where applicable.**
   To the extent permitted by applicable law, you waive or agree not to assert any moral rights that would prevent the project owner or a successor entity from using, modifying, relicensing, distributing, hosting, supporting, or commercializing bluei and official bluei-related offerings.

7. **No obligation to use your contribution.**
   The project owner is not required to accept, merge, publish, or maintain any contribution.

## Corporate Contributions

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer or organization, you represent that you are authorized to make the contribution under the terms of this CLA on behalf of that employer or organization.

## How To Agree

By opening a pull request, you agree to this CLA.

Maintainers may also ask contributors to include this statement in the PR description:

> I have read and agree to the CLA in `CLA.md`.

## Open-Source Commitment

The public bluei core is intended to remain open source. Copies already released under AGPL-3.0-or-later remain available under those terms.

The project owner may offer official hosted, commercial, enterprise, support, managed-service, or dual-licensed versions of bluei, but that does not remove the AGPL-licensed public releases already made available.
